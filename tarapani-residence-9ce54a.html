0 2 · Option A · The Product·DuraSeam 24Ga · Galvalume

One roof, folded from sheet steel.

24 gauge striated standing seam, 1.5″ rib, 15″ wide panels.

A standing seam roof has no exposed fasteners. Each panel locks into the next along a raised rib that sits an inch and a half above the water line, and the screws that hold it down are hidden underneath the seam. There is nothing on the surface for water to find and nothing to back out over time.

24 gauge is the heavier of the two common thicknesses. It resists oil-canning, the visible waviness that shows up on thinner panels across a long run, which matters on a building with roof planes this size. Galvalume is an aluminium-zinc coating over steel, and on a building three miles from open water that coating is doing real work every day.

The striated finish is the fine ribbing rolled into each panel between the seams. It stiffens the panel and breaks up reflections, so the roof reads as texture from the street rather than as a sheet of glare.

  5. 0 5

    High-temperature is the specific requirement under metal. A metal roof runs far hotter than the deck below a shingle roof, and standard underlayment softens and fails under it. This one also meets the secondary water resistance criteria on your wind mitigation form.

  6. 0 6

    Install new drip edge flashing to match metal system and properly hem panels around perimeter of roof.

    Hemming means the panel edge is folded back on itself around the whole perimeter. That folded edge is what stops wind getting a fingernail under a panel at the eaves and rakes, which is where a metal roof fails first if it fails at all.

  7. 0 7

    All penetrations to be flashed in accordance with manufacturer, SMACNA, and NRCA specifications.

    Three separate standards, not one. Every vent and every pipe gets a proper formed flashing rather than a bead of sealant that dries out in four summers.

  8. 0 8

    Seal all flashings where applicable with high grade polymer sealant.

    Applied over metal that is already correctly detailed, as a belt-and-braces layer. Sealant is never the waterproofing on this system, it is the backup to it.

  9. 0 9

    Install 24Ga DuraSeam striated panel over entire roof in accordance with manufacturer specification and approved shop drawings, preventing contact from dissimilar metals. Designed by the manufacturer for your home as a complete system. Panel dimensions 1.5 inch rib height and 15 inches wide. Colour: Galvalume.

    Dissimilar metal contact is the detail most people never hear about. Put steel against the wrong metal in salt air and you get galvanic corrosion, which eats the roof from the contact point outward. Every junction on this roof is detailed to prevent it.

    Behind the wall metal as well as under the roof. Water that gets past the face of a wall has somewhere to go and something to stop it.

0 4 · Option B · The Product·GAF EverGuard® TPO · 60 mil

A single welded sheet, and an inch of insulation under it.

GAF 60 mil EverGuard® or 50 mil Extreme, mechanically attached over 1″ ISO board.

TPO is a thermoplastic membrane. The sheets are heat-welded to each other rather than glued, so the seam is not a joint that can peel, it is the same material fused into one piece. On a flat roof, where water sits rather than runs off, the seam is the whole ballgame.

The part that separates this from a bare membrane is the 1″ ISO board underneath, at roughly R-6. A flat roof over occupied space is the largest uninsulated surface most buildings have. Adding a full inch of insulation between the deck and the membrane changes what the space below costs to cool, every month, for as long as the roof is up there.

TPO is white. On a Tarpon Springs summer that is not a cosmetic decision, it is the difference between a surface that reflects the afternoon and one that absorbs it and passes the heat down.
