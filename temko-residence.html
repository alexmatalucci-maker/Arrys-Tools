0 1 · The Product·Tilcor CF Shake · Charcoal Blend

Stone-coated steel,
pressed from New Zealand Steel.

The roof you pictured, built out of metal.

Tilcor is a stone-coated steel roofing system — a pressed ZINCALUME® steel panel, coated with natural stone granules bedded in an acrylic base coat, sealed under a clear over-glaze and oven-cured at 120°C. From the street it reads as a deep, textured shake roof. Structurally, it is a steel roof.

CF Shake is the deep-ribbed profile. It throws the strongest shadow line of the three Tilcor profiles, which is exactly what gives a white farmhouse its contrast. In Charcoal Blend — the darkest colour in the range — it delivers the roof in your rendering.

The “CF” stands for Concealed Fastening. Every screw is hidden beneath the course above it, out of sight and out of the weather. There is no exposed fastener on this roof to back out, wear its gasket, or weep a rust line down your new siding in year eight.

Your plans call for fiberglass asphalt dimensional shingles — a perfectly good code-compliant roof, and the shortest-lived component on a house you are building to keep. This is the one change we are proposing.

0 2 · The Scope·Labor & materials · complete system

Exactly what we install.

Nothing here is an allowance or a to-be-determined.

This is the full scope of work, as it appears in your contract.

  1. 0 1

    High-temperature peel & stick dry-in

    One full layer, mechanically fastened per current Florida and local building codes. This assembly meets the secondary water resistance criteria on your wind mitigation report — a line item your insurer will ask about.

  2. 0 2

    New drip edge to match the metal system

    Panels properly hemmed around the entire roof perimeter. Hemmed edges are what keep wind from getting a fingernail under the roof at the eaves and rakes.

  3. 0 3

    All penetrations flashed to three standards

    Manufacturer specification, SMACNA and NRCA. Plumbing vents, the tankless water heater vent, exhaust terminations and the chimney cap each get a proper flashing — not a bead of sealant.

  4. 0 4

    All flashings sealed with high-grade polymer

    Applied where applicable, over correctly detailed metal — as a belt-and-suspenders layer, never as the waterproofing itself.

  5. 0 5

    Tilcor CF Shake, Charcoal Blend, over the entire roof

    Installed per manufacturer specification and approved shop drawings, with dissimilar-metal contact prevented throughout. Designed by Tilcor for your home as a complete system — panels, hips, ridges, valleys and trims engineered together.

  6. 0 6

    Ridge vent sized for proper attic ventilation

    An adequate run per manufacturer specification. Your plans already call for decorative gable louvers; the ridge vent completes the intake-to-exhaust path that keeps the attic dry and the deck cool. Upgrades available.

  7. 0 7

    Roof accessories and details

    Installed per manufacturer specification and Metal Roofing Alliance recommendations, in compliance with Florida and local building codes.

  8. 0 8

    Warranty, cleanup and every fee

    20-year workmanship warranty plus all manufacturer warranties on final payment. Complimentary third-party wind mitigation report. All debris removed from rooftop and grounds. Permits, dump fees, workers' compensation, general liability and taxes are inside the price.

Why this is suddenly worth talking about

  1. 0 1

    The wind mitigation form changed

    Florida's wind mitigation inspection form was just updated for the first time in over twelve years — and the new version includes a section specifically for FORTIFIED certification. That form is what your carrier prices your wind coverage from.

  2. 0 2

    Homeowners nearby are seeing real reductions

    We can't promise a number, because every carrier and every policy is different. What we can tell you is what we are seeing locally — certified homeowners reporting savings in the 20–40% range on their wind coverage.

  3. 0 3

    Your roof's age stops being a reason to drop you

    Keep the certification current — it renews every five years — and your carrier cannot cancel your policy solely because of the age of your roof for up to twenty years. In this market, that is worth as much as the discount.

  4. 0 4

    Almost nobody around here can do it

    Arry's is one of only two roofing contractors in this area qualified to offer FORTIFIED certification. It requires certified crews and an independent inspection most contractors won't take on.

The best part: you're most of the way there

Arry's has always built roofs this way. The methods FORTIFIED requires are largely the methods already written into your contract — a fully sealed, dried-in deck, locked-down edges, and every detail installed to manufacturer specification.

The $5,000 doesn't buy a different roof. It buys the certification, documentation and independent inspection that turn the roof you are already getting into something your insurance company formally recognizes.

And it is cleanest to decide now, while the house is going up — the inspection depends on documenting work while it is still visible.
